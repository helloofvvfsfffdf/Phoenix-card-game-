      How it works
      
    
        
      
      • Cards come from a 40-card deck and everybody is dealt five. Each turn you draw one, then play one of your cards. When the deck runs out the discard pile is reshuffled into a new deck, so a game never stalls.
        • 
      
      • Five players, one turn each per round, in rotating order. The game runs for the round count set above and the highest score wins.
        • 
      
      • You play by clicking a card in your hand, then choosing a target if the card needs one. You draw one card at the start of your turn and play one of the cards you are holding.
        • 
      
      • ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Copilot are external AIs. When one of their turns comes the game waits. Copy the prompt into that AI, paste its reply back, and the game plays exactly what it chose. Nothing is played automatically and there are no network calls.
        • 
      
      • Eliminated for 1 round means you miss exactly one turn and are then back in play. An eliminated player still keeps their points.
        • 
      
      • Hunter does one thing only: if someone throws a Zombie at a Hunter, the thrower is the one eliminated. Knife and Steal Points still land normally on a Hunter.

# PHOENIXTube temporary video assets

These six clips are original, locally generated animated placeholder videos for
the PHOENIXTube release. Each is encoded at exactly 120 seconds and contains
moving branded visuals, captions, and playback controls. The challenge's
server-authoritative digit is overlaid only after the player reaches its hidden
timestamp; it is not baked into a filename, video, or metadata.

They are intentionally labelled temporary because they are simple generated
motion graphics rather than the final entertaining editorial videos. Replace
the six MP4s with appropriately licensed finished clips while preserving their
120-second duration and the source filenames.

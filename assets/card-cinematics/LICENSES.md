# Original PHOENIX card cinematic assets

The fourteen editable Effekseer projects, exported binaries, eight PNG textures
and gallery SVG emblems were authored specifically for this PHOENIX preview.
No downloaded sample effect binaries, stock particle packs or third-party images
are included in this directory. `scripts/build-card-cinematics.js` is the
reproducible texture/project generator and invokes the installed Effekseer editor
to compile the runtime effects. Originals in Downloads were not changed.

The preview reuses the already bundled Effekseer 1.80.7 and GSAP 3.15.0 runtimes
under `assets/victory/vendor/`. Their upstream licences and notices remain in
`assets/victory/LICENSES.md`. Effekseer: MIT; GSAP: its bundled standard licence.
The victory effect assets themselves are not used or modified by this gallery.

No audio assets are added, requested or played by the gallery.
